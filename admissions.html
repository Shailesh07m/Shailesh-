Admissions 2024-25

Your Journey to Excellence Starts Here

Join our prestigious institution and become part of a community that values academic excellence, character development, and holistic growth.

Admission Process

Step 1: Application

Fill out the online application form with accurate personal and academic information. Ensure all required fields are completed.

Step 2: Document Submission

Upload required documents including mark sheets, certificates, and photographs. Ensure all documents are clear and legible.

Step 3: Verification

Our admission team will verify your documents and eligibility. You may be called for an interview or counseling session.

Step 4: Admission Confirmation

Upon successful verification, complete the admission process by paying fees and confirming your enrollment.
