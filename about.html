About Our Institution

Our Story

Established in 1995, Bhartiya Jain Sangathan (BJS) College has been a beacon of quality education in Wagholi, Pune. Founded on the principles of Jain philosophy emphasizing non-violence, truthfulness, and compassion, our institution has grown to become one of the most respected educational establishments in the region.

Over nearly three decades, we have nurtured thousands of students, helping them realize their potential and achieve their dreams. Our commitment to academic excellence, combined with strong moral values, has made BJS College a preferred choice for students seeking holistic education.
