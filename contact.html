Find Us on Map

Located in the heart of Wagholi, our campus is easily accessible by public transport and private vehicles.

Public Transport

Regular bus services available from Pune Station and major areas. Wagholi Bus Stand is just 2 minutes walk from campus.

By Car

Easy access via Nagar Road and Wagholi Road. Ample parking space available on campus for students and visitors.

Nearest Railway

Pune Railway Station is 20 km away. Local trains and buses provide regular connectivity to Wagholi from the station.
