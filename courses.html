Our Academic Programs

Discover Your Path to Success

Choose from our comprehensive range of undergraduate courses designed to provide quality education and prepare you for a successful career.

Commerce Stream

Our Commerce programs are designed to build strong foundations in business, accounting, and economics to prepare students for the dynamic business world.

Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com)

A comprehensive program covering accounting, business law, economics, and management principles. This course provides a strong foundation for careers in finance, banking, and business management.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Completed 12th standard from a recognized board
  • Minimum 50% marks in aggregate (45% for reserved categories)
  • Mathematics/Commerce background preferred

Career Prospects:

  • Chartered Accountant (CA)
  • Company Secretary (CS)
  • Banking & Finance Professional
  • Business Analyst
  • Tax Consultant
  • Government Services (Banking Exams)

Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)

An industry-oriented program focusing on management skills, leadership development, and business strategy. Ideal for students aspiring to become business leaders and entrepreneurs.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Completed 12th standard from any recognized board
  • Minimum 50% marks in aggregate
  • Any stream background acceptable
  • Entrance test may be required

Career Prospects:

  • Business Manager
  • Marketing Executive
  • HR Professional
  • Entrepreneur
  • Sales Manager
  • Project Manager

Bachelor of Commerce in Accounting & Finance (B.Com A&F)

Specialized program in accounting and finance with emphasis on financial management, auditing, and taxation. Perfect for students interested in financial careers.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Completed 12th with Commerce/Mathematics
  • Minimum 55% marks in aggregate
  • Knowledge of basic accounting preferred

Career Prospects:

  • Financial Analyst
  • Investment Banker
  • Audit Executive
  • Tax Officer
  • Corporate Finance Manager
  • Risk Analyst

Science Stream

Our Science programs offer cutting-edge education in various scientific disciplines, preparing students for research, technology, and innovation-driven careers.

Bachelor of Science in Computer Science (B.Sc CS)

Comprehensive program covering programming, software development, data structures, and emerging technologies. Designed to create skilled IT professionals for the digital age.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Completed 12th with Science (PCM/PCB)
  • Minimum 50% marks in aggregate
  • Mathematics mandatory
  • Basic computer knowledge preferred

Career Prospects:

  • Software Developer
  • Web Developer
  • Data Scientist
  • System Administrator
  • Cybersecurity Specialist
  • AI/ML Engineer

Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (B.Sc IT)

Focused on information systems, database management, networking, and IT infrastructure. Ideal for students interested in IT services and technology management.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Completed 12th with Science/Commerce
  • Minimum 50% marks in aggregate
  • Mathematics at 10+2 level
  • Interest in technology and computers

Career Prospects:

  • IT Consultant
  • Network Administrator
  • Database Administrator
  • IT Project Manager
  • Systems Analyst
  • Technical Support Specialist

Bachelor of Science in Biotechnology (B.Sc Biotech)

Interdisciplinary program combining biology, chemistry, and technology. Focuses on genetic engineering, molecular biology, and biomedical applications.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Completed 12th with Science (PCB/PCM)
  • Minimum 55% marks in Biology/Chemistry
  • Interest in biological sciences
  • Laboratory experience preferred

Career Prospects:

  • Biotechnology Researcher
  • Quality Control Analyst
  • Pharmaceutical Scientist
  • Clinical Research Associate
  • Biomedical Engineer
  • Environmental Scientist

Bachelor of Science in Mathematics (B.Sc Maths)

Pure and applied mathematics program covering algebra, calculus, statistics, and mathematical modeling. Foundation for careers in research, analytics, and academia.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Completed 12th with Science (PCM)
  • Minimum 60% marks in Mathematics
  • Strong analytical skills
  • Interest in problem-solving

Career Prospects:

  • Data Analyst
  • Statistician
  • Research Scientist
  • Actuary
  • Mathematics Teacher
  • Financial Analyst

Arts Stream

Our Arts programs foster creativity, critical thinking, and cultural understanding. These courses prepare students for diverse careers in humanities, social sciences, and creative fields.

Bachelor of Arts (B.A.)

Comprehensive liberal arts education with choice of subjects including Literature, History, Political Science, Sociology, and Economics. Develops critical thinking and communication skills.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Completed 12th from any recognized board
  • Minimum 45% marks in aggregate
  • Any stream background acceptable
  • Language proficiency preferred

Career Prospects:

  • Civil Services (IAS/IPS)
  • Journalism & Media
  • Teaching & Education
  • Content Writing
  • Social Work
  • Public Relations

Bachelor of Arts in Psychology (B.A. Psychology)

Scientific study of human behavior and mental processes. Covers cognitive psychology, developmental psychology, and counseling techniques. Ideal for future psychologists and counselors.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Completed 12th from any stream
  • Minimum 50% marks in aggregate
  • Interest in human behavior
  • Good observation skills

Career Prospects:

  • Clinical Psychologist
  • Counselor
  • HR Professional
  • Social Worker
  • Research Psychologist
  • Organizational Psychologist

Bachelor of Arts in Mass Communication (B.A. Mass Comm)

Comprehensive program covering journalism, advertising, public relations, and digital media. Designed for creative minds interested in media and communication.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Completed 12th from any stream
  • Minimum 50% marks in aggregate
  • Good communication skills
  • Creative writing ability

Career Prospects:

  • Journalist
  • News Anchor
  • Content Creator
  • Advertising Executive
  • PR Specialist
  • Film/TV Producer

Bachelor of Social Work (BSW)

Professional program focused on social justice, community development, and human welfare. Prepares students to address social issues and work with diverse populations.

Eligibility Criteria:

  • Completed 12th from any stream
  • Minimum 45% marks in aggregate
  • Interest in social service
  • Empathy and communication skills

Career Prospects:

  • Social Worker
  • NGO Program Manager
  • Community Development Officer
  • Healthcare Social Worker
  • Policy Analyst
  • Child Welfare Specialist
